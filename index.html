Add it to your site

Paste before </body>:

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/SumOfficeApp/layers-embed@v1/embed.js" defer></script>

Then mark a file link, or let every office link get the button:

<a data-layers href="/files/contract.docx">Contract</a>

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/SumOfficeApp/layers-embed@v1/embed.js" data-links="all" defer></script>

A live document from the catalog of 4,000+ letters, contracts and calculators:

<div data-layers-form="freelance-invoice-payment-reminder" data-height="760"></div>

Works with plain HTML, Ghost, Webflow, Squarespace, Docusaurus, MkDocs, Hugo, Jekyll and any page that accepts a script tag. The file must be reachable by a public link.

What your visitor gets

  • The document opens as a fast page, on a phone too.
  • They fill in their part and send it for signature. The other side signs from a link.
  • They save a finished copy to their own Layers account.
